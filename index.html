PIN 02 · ABOUT

About me

I'm a first-year student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and I plan to study Electrical Engineering. The part of EE I'm most curious about is semiconductors: how a slab of doped silicon turns into a transistor, a sensor, or an LED.

This semester I'm teaching myself embedded systems on an STM32 "Blue Pill" board, starting from a single blinking LED and keeping a public log of every problem I hit and how I fixed it. My goal for Spring 2027 is an LED I-V curve tracer that connects that embedded work back to semiconductor physics.

Outside of hardware, I build software when I run into a problem I want solved. At a hackathon I helped build F1 Compass, which helps international students check what their visa rules allow, and I made Study Tracker because I wanted to see where my study hours were actually going.

PIN 04 · PROJECTS

Projects

F1 Compass

Hackathon · team project

Know before you work. A web app that tells international students on F-1 visas whether an activity (freelancing, a campus job, a hackathon prize) is likely allowed, with citations from official U.S. government sources.

  • A student asks a question in plain language; NVIDIA Nemotron models on Nebius Token Factory plan the search, Tavily searches only official sites like uscis.gov and ecfr.gov, and a reasoning model returns a color-coded verdict.
  • Every answer includes the legal paths (CPT / OPT), questions to ask the school's DSO, and a ready-to-send email.
  • Tested against a 30-question answer key: 25/30 correct, with results tracked for "dangerous" mistakes.
  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • NVIDIA Nemotron
  • Tavily
  • Vercel

Study Tracker

Personal project

See where your study hours go. A study timer that tracks focused time per course and helps plan the week ahead.

  • Focus timer with pause, per-course pages with to-dos, and a weekly study planner.
  • Exports sessions to a calendar file (.ics) or CSV, supports study groups and a map of favorite study spots, and plays a recap of your progress.
  • Installable as a Progressive Web App with a service worker, so it opens offline like a phone app.
